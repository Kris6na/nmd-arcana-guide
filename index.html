Ninja Must Die: Arcana System

This guide focuses exclusively on High-Tier Arcana.

Element colors: Wind · Lightning · Water · Fire · All Ninjas

🔎 At a glance

  • Arcana is a battle buff system. Equip a full set of 4 runes to unlock a skill that triggers in Boss battles.
  • Each rune gives stats (always active) and attributes (active only when the skill activates).
  • A skill activates once its countdown has finished and its condition is met. It activates once per battle unless attributes add more.

⚠️ Data note: The ranges in this guide are based on observation of thousands of runes. If you get values outside them, you are either very lucky or very unlucky.

1. How Arcana Works

💡 Key Terms

  • Arcana skill: the special move that triggers during Boss battles.
  • Rune set: the 4 runes (one per element) needed to activate an arcana skill.
  • Activation: the skill triggering, once its condition is met and its countdown has finished.
  • Tier: Low, Mid or High. Higher tiers need more specific runes and Ninjas.
  • Arcana is a battle buff system built around 4 rune slots.
  • Equipping a full rune set unlocks a special skill that triggers during Boss battles.
  • A rune set also gives stat bonuses and attribute effects from the runes themselves.

1.1 Arcana Tiers and Activation Requirements

Arcana is divided into Low, Mid, and High tiers:

  • Low-tier arcana skills: Require 2 low-tier runes to activate. No Ninja restrictions.
  • Mid-tier arcana skills: Require 3 mid-tier runes to activate. No Ninja restrictions. The exception is mid-tier Divine Blessing, which requires 4 runes.
  • High-tier arcana skills: Require 4 high-tier runes to activate. A Ninja of the matching element is required. The exception is high-tier Divine Blessing, which has no Ninja restriction.

1.2 Rune Sets

  • Every arcana skill requires a rune set to activate. A high-tier rune set is 4 runes of the same arcana skill, one of each element (Wind, Lightning, Water, Fire).
  • An incomplete or incorrect combination of runes will not activate the arcana skill.

What still works without a complete, correct set:

FeatureComplete setIncomplete or incorrect set
Rune Stats (I) and (II)✅ Work✅ Work
Rune Attributes (I) and (II)✅ Work once the skill activates❌ Ineffective
Arcana skill✅ Activates❌ Does not activate

Rune Stats work for whichever Ninja you have deployed, whatever its element.

2. High-Tier Arcana Skills

💡 Key Terms

  • Screen Sweep: a hit that covers the whole screen. The damage it deals to the Boss varies depending on the skill.
  • Screen clearing: Roar and Screen Sweep may not clear every projectile, especially when the Boss has an indestructible attack pattern.

2.1 Kamaitachi (Wind Element Ninja Only)

  • Skill Effect: Summons Kamaitachi to continuously fire wind blades at the Boss, then charges toward the Boss dealing collision DMG.
  • Activation Condition: Use your main weapon skill twice.
  • Countdown: 55 seconds in a Boss battle. Weapon uses accumulate during the countdown.

2.2 Beast Bull (Lightning Element Ninja Only)

  • Skill Effect: Summons Minotaur to charge back and forth once, stop in front of the Ninja to roar, and dash away.
  • Activation Condition: Activates automatically once the countdown finishes.
  • Countdown: 60 seconds in a Boss battle.

2.3 Blowfish (Water Element Ninja Only)

  • Skill Effect: Summons Blowfish to block damage in front for 4s, then deals Screen Sweep DMG. The sweep damage scales based on the damage blocked.
  • Activation Condition: Accumulate 500 points of damage taken from the Boss.
  • Countdown: 55 seconds in a Boss battle. Damage taken accumulates during the countdown.

2.4 Candle Dragon (Fire Element Ninja Only)

  • Skill Effect: Summons Candle Dragon to fire 4 fireballs forward, then charges toward the Boss dealing collision DMG.
  • Activation Condition: Play without taking damage in a Boss battle.
  • Countdown: 50 seconds in a Boss battle. Taking damage pauses the countdown for 5s rather than resetting it.

2.5 Divine Blessing (All Ninjas)

  • Skill Effect: The Ninja grows in size and gains temporary invincibility, releasing a Screen Sweep effect when invincibility ends.
  • Activation Condition: HP drops below 50% in a Boss battle.
  • Countdown: 60 seconds in a Boss battle.

3. Activation Mechanics

💡 Key Terms

  • Activation condition: what has to happen in battle before the skill is allowed to activate (for example, taking no damage).
  • Countdown: a timer that starts when the Boss HP bar appears. The skill activates only when the countdown has finished and the condition is met.
  • Relay: switching to another Ninja during battle. See 3.3 Relay for how it affects countdowns.

3.1 Activation Basics

  • Every arcana skill activates once per battle by default, once its countdown has finished and its activation condition is met.
  • Both the condition and the countdown must be fulfilled. Each skill has its own (see Section 2).
  • Each arcana skill requires its rune set to activate (see 1.2 Rune Sets).
  • Some rune attributes add bonus activations (see 3.4 Bonus Activations).

3.2 Countdown and Condition

  • Start: The countdown starts when the Boss HP bar appears, as long as your Ninja is the correct element for the skill. Without the correct element Ninja, the countdown won’t start.
  • Both are required: You can meet the condition early, but the skill still waits for the countdown to end. If you haven’t met the condition by then, it activates as soon as you do.
  • Accumulating conditions: Kamaitachi’s weapon uses and Blowfish’s damage taken both accumulate during the countdown.
  • Candle Dragon: Taking damage pauses the countdown for 5s instead of resetting it.

3.3 Relay

Relaying during a battle also affects your arcana skill’s countdowns. What happens depends on when you relay:

When you relayWhat happens
Before the skill has activated for the first time The first activation’s countdown restarts. The additional activation’s countdown only starts after the first activation, so it isn’t affected yet.
After the first activation, before the additional activation The relay restarts the additional activation’s countdown.
After the skill has used all its activations No countdown is left to restart. A relay can still activate the skill if you have the Skill Activation on Relay attribute.
2nd relay No further effect.

3.4 Bonus Activations

  • Once per battle is the default. Two rune attributes add bonus activations on top of it: Additional Skill Activation and Skill Activation on Relay.
  • Additional Skill Activation has its own countdown. It starts only after the first activation, and it’s a separate wait from the first one.
  • Condition for the additional activation: You must meet the activation condition again, because it resets along with the countdown.
  • Skill Activation on Relay ignores the condition. It also doesn’t wait on any countdown, so it activates on your 1st relay no matter which element Ninja you started with or relay into.

4. Runes

💡 Key Terms: Stat vs. Attribute

  • Stat: a plain numeric bonus on the rune, such as ATK or Max HP. Stats always work for the deployed Ninja, even with an incomplete set.
  • Attribute: a special effect that modifies the arcana skill, such as an extra activation or bonus crit. Attributes only work once the skill activates.

4.1 Rune Elements and Sources

Runes come in 4 elements:

  • Wind
  • Lightning
  • Water
  • Fire

You can get runes from a weekly event called 4 Symbols Exploration.

4.2 Leveling Milestones

Runes can be leveled from Lv. 1 to Lv. 10. Leveling up runes increases base stats and unlocks additional Rune Attributes and Rune Stats at specific levels.

LevelUnlocksWhat it gives
Lv. 1Rune Stat (I) and Rune Attribute (I)+50 elemental ATK (grows with level), plus a random attribute from First, Second or Third Class
Lv. 5Rune Attribute (II)A random attribute from Second or Third Class
Lv. 8Rune Stat (II)A random stat that does not grow with level

4.3 Rune Stats

  • Rune Stat (I) (unlocks at Lv. 1): Gain +50 elemental ATK. Increases by +50 at every level up, up to a max of 500.
  • Rune Stat (II) (unlocks at Lv. 8): Gain a random stat. It does not increase with level up. It stays at what it rolled, including past Lv. 8.
  • Lv.8 stats pool: ATK increase, Weapon ATK increase, Ninjutsu ATK increase, Max HP increase, Score Bonus, Effect Resistance.
  • Rune Stats work for any deployed Ninja, even with an incomplete or incorrect set (see 1.2 Rune Sets).

4.4 Rune Attribute Slots

  • Rune Attribute (I) (unlocks at Lv. 1): Gain a random attribute from First Class, Second Class, or Third Class.
  • Rune Attribute (II) (unlocks at Lv. 5): Gain another random attribute from Second Class or Third Class. First Class attributes can’t roll at this level.
  • Attribute (II) can roll the same attribute as Attribute (I), but only Second and Third Class attributes can be duplicated this way.
  • The First Class and Second Class attribute pools are limited by the element of the rune.
  • Attributes stay ineffective without arcana skill activation (see 1.2 Rune Sets). Full details are in Section 5.

4.5 Upgrading and Dismantling

4.5.1 Upgrade Costs

  • Certain materials are used to level up runes.
  • The material cost to level up a rune increases with each level.

4.5.2 Dismantle System and Refund Rate

  • Unwanted or poorly rolled runes can be dismantled for upgrade materials.
  • Dismantling a Lv. 1 rune yields a fixed base amount of materials.
  • Leveled-up runes return the base amount plus 80% of the upgrade materials spent on them.

5. Rune Attributes

💡 Key Terms: Class and Pool

  • Class: a convenient classification of all available attributes for all runes. This is based on attribute rarity and impact upon gameplay.
  • Pool: the list of attributes a slot can roll from. Some pools are limited by the rune’s element.
  • Stack: identical attributes add together. Almost all identical attributes equipped in a rune set stack together.

Example: Attribute (II) draws from the Second and Third Class pools. Second Class is limited to the rune’s element, and Third Class is open to every element.

5.1 First Class Attribute Pool

  • Can be obtained from specific element runes only.
  • Can be equipped in the Rune Attribute (I) slot only.
  • Can be obtained at rune Level 1 only.

5.1.1 Additional Skill Activation (Wind Element Runes Only)

  • Grants +1 arcana skill activation per battle at 20% to 50% of original skill DMG.
  • The additional activation has its own countdown, which starts after the first activation.
  • All equipped Second Class and Third Class rune attributes take effect upon skill activation.

5.1.2 Skill Activation on Relay (Lightning Element Runes Only)

  • Immediately triggers the arcana skill once upon your 1st relay at 50% to 100% of original DMG.
  • All equipped Second Class and Third Class rune attributes take effect upon skill activation.
  • Does not trigger on a 2nd relay.

5.1.3 Skill Countdown Reduction (Water Element Runes Only)

  • Reduces the skill’s Boss battle countdown by 5s to 12s.
  • Also shortens the additional activation countdown.

5.1.4 Skill Upgrade (Fire Element Runes Only)

Enhances the active effect of the specific arcana skill:

  • Kamaitachi: Increases Wind Blade release duration by +0.5s to +1.5s.
  • Beast Bull: Increases Roar DMG. The Roar can clear the screen.
  • Blowfish: Increases Screen Sweep DMG at the end of the skill.
  • Candle Dragon: Releases +1 to +2 extra Fireballs.
  • Divine Blessing: Increases invincibility duration by +0.5s to +1.5s.

5.2 Second Class Attribute Pool

  • Can be obtained from specific element runes only.
  • Can be equipped in the Rune Attribute (I) and (II) slots.
  • Can be obtained at rune Level 1 and 5 only.

5.2.1 Permanent ATK Boost (Wind Element Runes Only)

  • Gain a permanent stack of +50 to +120 ATK after the first skill activation.
  • Can stack with identical attributes. No known cap.
  • Additional skill activations do not add more stacks. It only stacks once from the first skill activation.

5.2.2 Skill Crit DMG (Lightning Element Runes Only)

  • Gain +14% to +30% Crit DMG for the arcana skill.
  • Can stack with identical attributes. No known cap.

5.2.3 Skill DMG Increase (Water Element Runes Only)

  • Gain +10% to +15% DMG for the arcana skill.
  • Can stack with identical attributes. No known cap.

5.2.4 Skill Crit Rate (Fire Element Runes Only)

  • Gain +5% to +25% Crit Rate for the arcana skill.
  • Can stack with identical attributes. No known cap.

5.3 Third Class Attribute Pool

  • Can be obtained from all element runes.
  • Can be equipped in the Rune Attribute (I) and (II) slots.
  • Can be obtained at rune Level 1 and 5 only.

5.3.1 Temporary ATK Boost (for 10 seconds)

  • Beast Bull, Blowfish, Divine Blessing: Gain +50 to +120 ATK on skill activation.
  • Candle Dragon: Gain +6 to +20 ATK per Fireball hit.
  • Kamaitachi: Gain +2 to +8 ATK per Wind Blade hit.
  • Can stack with identical attributes.
  • Capped at 400 ATK max for 10 seconds.

5.3.2 HP Recovery / Instant Heal

  • Recover +50 to +120 HP immediately upon activation.
  • Can stack with identical attributes.
  • Capped at 500 HP per activation.

5.3.3 HP Recovery Speed / Regeneration (for 10 seconds)

  • Recover +5 to +10 HP/s continuously for 10 seconds.
  • Can stack with identical attributes. No known cap.

5.3.4 Effect Resistance

  • Gain +5% to +8% debuff resistance.
  • Can stack with identical attributes. No known cap.

6. FAQ

Short answers, with links to the full explanation.

Q: The skill activates “once per battle,” but I’ve seen it activate more than once. Why?
Rune attributes can add bonus activations. See 3.4 Bonus Activations.
Q: What does a relay do to my skill?
Your 1st relay restarts the running countdown, and your 2nd has no further effect. See 3.3 Relay.
Q: What happens if my Ninja’s element doesn’t match the skill?
The countdown won’t start. See 3.2 Countdown and Condition.
Q: Do runes still help if I don’t have a complete set?
Only partly: Rune Stats work, Rune Attributes don’t. See 1.2 Rune Sets.
Q: Do Rune Stats only work for a Ninja of the matching element?
No. They work for any deployed Ninja. See 4.3 Rune Stats.
Q: Can my Level 5 rune give a First Class attribute? Can it match my Level 1 attribute?
No to the first. Yes to the second, but only for Second and Third Class attributes. See 4.4 Rune Attribute Slots.
Q: Does Rune Stat (II) get stronger when I level the rune past 8?
No. See 4.3 Rune Stats.
Q: Can I stack identical attributes?
Yes, for the Second and Third Class attributes listed with that rule. No cap is known for most of them. The exceptions are Temporary ATK Boost (capped at 400 ATK max for 10 seconds) and HP Recovery / Instant Heal (capped at 500 HP per activation). See Section 5.
Q: Do bonus activations add more stacks of Permanent ATK Boost?
No. See 5.2.1.
Q: How much do I get back from dismantling a rune?
See 4.5.2 Dismantle System and Refund Rate.
