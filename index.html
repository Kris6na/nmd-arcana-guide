High-Tier Arcana System Guide

A complete breakdown of High-Tier (4-Rune) Arcana sets—covering weekly 4SE portal farming, Rune upgrade milestones from Lv. 1 to Lv. 10, battle stacking limits, and full elemental pools for all five sets.

Guide Scope & Exclusions

  • Included: Verified mechanics, stat pools, roll ranges, and battle caps for all 5 High-Tier (4-Rune) Arcana Sets.
  • Unavailable / Excluded: Low-Tier (2-Rune) and Mid-Tier (3-Rune / 4-Rune) Arcana sets are not covered here. Unconfirmed RNG hypotheses and subjective tier lists are also excluded.

1. How the Arcana System Works

Arcana is a battle buff system built around 4 Rune slots. Understanding how tiers, weekly farming portals, and set activation rules interact prevents wasted upgrade materials early on.

Arcana Tiers

  • Low-Tier Sets: Require 2 Runes to activate (excluded from this guide).
  • Mid-Tier Sets: Require 3 Runes to activate, except Mid-Tier Divine Blessing which requires 4 Runes (excluded from this guide).
  • High-Tier Sets: Require 4 Runes to activate and are the sole focus of this guide.

Weekly Rune Farming (4SE)

  • Weekly Mode: High-Tier Runes drop from the weekly battle mode 4SE.
  • Rotating Elemental Portals: Each week, 4SE opens 2 portals, each dedicated to 1 of the 4 elements (Wind, Lightning, Water, or Fire).
  • Portal Entry Restriction: You must enter each portal using Ninjas of the matching element. Defeating the portal's bosses drops Runes of that specific element.

Activating a High-Tier Set

  • 4-Element Match: Equip 4 High-Tier Runes of the exact same set name—one from each element (Wind, Lightning, Water, and Fire).
    • Mixing Runes from different sets prevents the Arcana Set Skill from activating at all.
  • Ninja Element Lock: The 4 offensive sets require deploying a Ninja of the matching element, whereas Divine Blessing works with any Ninja.
  • Default Trigger Limit: Every Set Skill triggers once per battle by default unless extended by a Wind or Lightning Special Effect.
Tier Mixing Warning: Divine Blessing exists in both a Mid-Tier version (+30 to +300 Element ATK) and a High-Tier version (+50 to +500 Element ATK). Never mix Mid-Tier and High-Tier Runes, or the set will not activate.

2. Rune Anatomy & Upgrade Milestones (Lv. 1 – Lv. 10)

Every High-Tier Rune is divided into Base Stats (always active from the start of battle) and Rune Effects (dormant until your Arcana Set triggers for the first time in battle). All Rune Effect values and Lv. 8 Secondary Base Stat values are RNG-generated within fixed ranges.

Base Stats Active from Battle Start

  • Primary Base Stat (Lv. 1 – Lv. 10):
    • Fixed [Element] Element ATK starting at +50 at Lv. 1.
    • Scales by +50 per level up to +500 at Lv. 10 (+2,000 total Element ATK across 4 maxed Runes).
  • Secondary Base Stat (Unlocks at Lv. 8):
    • Unlocks 1 permanent passive stat drawn randomly from a 6-stat pool: Raw ATK, Weapon ATK, Ninjutsu ATK, Max HP, Effect Resistance (Effect %), or Score Bonus (Score Bonus %).
    • Remains fixed at its initial Lv. 8 roll when upgrading further to Lv. 9–10.

Rune Effects Dormant Until 1st Set Trigger

  • Effect Slot 1 (Unlocked at Lv. 1):
    • Rolls either that element's Special Effect OR a Common Effect from that Set × Element pool.
  • Effect Slot 2 (Unlocked at Lv. 5):
    • Unlocks a second effect line drawn strictly from the Common Effect pool (never a Special Effect).
    • Uses the exact same RNG roll range as Lv. 1 and can duplicate Slot 1's Common Effect.

3. The 5 High-Tier Arcana Sets

Each High-Tier set has a unique trigger condition, Set Skill behavior, and 4 elemental Rune pools. Every Rune draws from its element's Lv. 1 Special Effect alongside its Damage and Regular / Filler Common Effect pools.

4. Common Effects & Set-Wide Caps

Common Effects can roll in either Slot 1 (Lv. 1) or Slot 2 (Lv. 5). Each element's Common pool consists of 1 Element-Exclusive Multiplier/ATK stat plus 4 Universal Cross-Element Utility stats.

5. Battle & Relay Interactions

Bonus triggers from Wind and Lightning Special Effects interact with your set countdown timer and Common Effects in specific ways:

First Relay Activation & Timer Reset

  • Relaying into your 2nd Ninja with Lightning's Activation on Relay fires the Set Skill immediately and activates all Common Effects (including Permanent ATK).
  • Simultaneously, it resets your normal set countdown timer to 100%, keeping your regular scheduled trigger available later in the battle.

Relay Limit in Boss Battles

  • Standard Boss Fights: Allow 1 relay.
  • Ultra-High-HP Boss Events: Allow up to 2 relays—the 2nd relay grants no relay activation bonus and simply continues the running countdown timer.

Bonus Activation Re-Triggers

  • When Wind (Additional Activation +1) or Lightning (Activation on Relay) triggers the set again, all Common Effects re-trigger—healing again and refreshing or stacking 10s buffs up to their set-wide caps.
  • Permanent ATK Exception: Permanent ATK Increase does not re-stack on bonus activations; it remains at 1 stack per equipped effect line for the rest of the battle.
