## 1. Arcana System

* Arcana is a **battle buff system** built around **4 rune slots**.
* Equipping a full rune set unlocks a special skill that triggers during Boss battles.
* A rune set also gives stat bonuses and attribute effects from the runes themselves.

### 1.1 Arcana Tiers and Activation Requirements

Arcana is divided into **Low**, **Mid**, and **High** tiers:

* **Low-tier arcana skills:** Require 2 low-tier runes to activate. No Ninja restrictions.
* **Mid-tier arcana skills:** Require 3 mid-tier runes to activate. No Ninja restrictions. The exception is mid-tier Divine Blessing, which requires 4 runes.
* **High-tier arcana skills:** Require 4 high-tier runes to activate. A Ninja of the matching element is required. The exception is high-tier Divine Blessing, which has no Ninja restriction.

### 1.2 Activation Basics

* Every arcana skill activates **once per battle** by default, once its countdown has finished and its activation condition is met.
* Your **1st relay** always restarts the current countdown. A 2nd relay has no effect.
* Some rune attributes add bonus activations (see [Section 4.1](#41-first-class-attributes)).
* Each arcana skill requires its **rune set** to activate (see [Section 3](#3-runes)).
* Each skill has its own trigger condition and countdown (see [Section 2](#2-high-tier-arcana-skills)). Both must be fulfilled to activate the skill.

## 2. High-Tier Arcana Skills

### 2.1 Kamaitachi (Wind Element Ninja Only)

* **Skill Effect:** Summons Kamaitachi to continuously fire **wind blades** at the Boss, then **charges** toward the Boss dealing collision DMG.
* **Activation Condition:** Use your **main weapon skill twice**.
* **Countdown:** **55 seconds** in a Boss battle. Weapon uses accumulate during the countdown.

### 2.2 Beast Bull (Lightning Element Ninja Only)

* **Skill Effect:** Summons Minotaur to charge back and forth once, stop in front of the Ninja to **roar**, and dash away.
* **Activation Condition:** Activates **automatically** once the countdown finishes.
* **Countdown:** **60 seconds** in a Boss battle.

### 2.3 Blowfish (Water Element Ninja Only)

* **Skill Effect:** Summons Blowfish to **block damage in front for 4s**, then deals **Screen Sweep DMG**. The sweep damage scales based on the damage blocked.
* **Activation Condition:** Accumulate **500 points of damage taken** from the Boss.
* **Countdown:** **55 seconds** in a Boss battle. Damage taken accumulates during the countdown.

### 2.4 Candle Dragon (Fire Element Ninja Only)

* **Skill Effect:** Summons Candle Dragon to fire **4 fireballs** forward, then **charges** toward the Boss dealing collision DMG.
* **Activation Condition:** Play **without taking damage** in a Boss battle.
* **Countdown:** **50 seconds** in a Boss battle. Taking damage pauses the countdown for 5s rather than resetting it.

### 2.5 Divine Blessing (All Ninjas)

* **Skill Effect:** The Ninja grows in size and gains **temporary invincibility**, releasing a **Screen Sweep** effect when invincibility ends.
* **Activation Condition:** **HP drops below 50%** in a Boss battle.
* **Countdown:** **60 seconds** in a Boss battle.

## 3. Runes

* Every arcana skill requires a rune set to activate. A high-tier rune set is 4 runes of the same arcana skill, one of each element (Wind, Lightning, Water, Fire).
* An incomplete or incorrect combination of runes will not activate the arcana skill.
* Rune Attributes (I) and (II) remain ineffective without arcana skill activation.
* Rune Stats (I) and (II) are effective even if the rune combination is incorrect or incomplete. They work for any Ninja who is deployed at the time, whatever its element.

### 3.1 Rune Elements and Sources

Runes come in 4 elements:

* **Wind**
* **Lightning**
* **Water**
* **Fire**

Runes can be obtained from a weekly event called **4 Symbols Exploration**.

### 3.2 Leveling Up Runes

Runes can be leveled from **Lv. 1 to Lv. 10**. Leveling up runes increases base stats and unlocks additional **Rune Attributes** and **Rune Stats** at specific levels.

#### 3.2.1 Level 1

* Unlocks the Rune Stat (I) slot and the Rune Attribute (I) slot.
* **Rune Stat (I):** Gain +50 elemental ATK. Increases by +50 at every level up, up to a max of 500.
* **Rune Attribute (I):** Gain a random attribute from First Class, Second Class, or Third Class.
* The First Class and Second Class attribute pool is limited by element of the Rune.

#### 3.2.2 Level 5

* Unlocks the Rune Attribute (II) slot.
* **Rune Attribute (II):** Gain another random attribute from Second Class or Third Class.
* You cannot obtain a First Class attribute at this level.
* The econd Class attribute pool is limited by element of the Rune.

#### 3.2.3 Level 8

* Unlocks the Rune Stat (II) slot.
* **Rune Stat (II):** Gain a random stat. It does not increase with level up.
* Possible stats: ATK increase, Weapon ATK increase, Ninjutsu ATK increase, Max HP increase, Score Bonus, Effect Resistance.
* Only Score Bonus and Effect Resistance are percentages. The rest are flat values.

## 4. Rune Attribute Classes

### 4.1 First Class Attribute Pool

* Can be obtained from specific element runes only.
* Can be equipped in the Rune Attribute (I) slot only.
* Can be obtained at rune Level 1 only.

#### 4.1.1 Additional Skill Activation (Wind Element Runes Only)

* Grants **+1 arcana skill activation** per battle at **20% to 50%** of original skill DMG.
* The additional activation has its own countdown, which starts after the first activation.
* All equipped **Second Class** and **Third Class** rune attributes take effect upon skill activation.

#### 4.1.2 Skill Activation on Relay (Lightning Element Runes Only)

* Immediately triggers the arcana skill once upon your **1st relay** at **50% to 100%** of original DMG.
* All equipped **Second Class** and **Third Class** rune attributes take effect upon skill activation.
* Does not trigger on a 2nd relay.

#### 4.1.3 Skill Countdown Reduction (Water Element Runes Only)

* Reduces the skill's Boss battle countdown by **5s to 12s**.
* Also shortens the additional activation countdown.

#### 4.1.4 Skill Upgrade (Fire Element Runes Only)

Enhances the active effect of the specific arcana skill:

* **Kamaitachi:** Increases Wind Blade release duration by **+0.5s to +1.5s**.
* **Beast Bull:** Increases **Roar DMG**. The Roar can clear the screen.
* **Blowfish:** Increases **Screen Sweep DMG** at the end of the skill.
* **Candle Dragon:** Releases **+1 to +2 extra Fireballs**.
* **Divine Blessing:** Increases invincibility duration by **+0.5s to +1.5s**.

### 4.2 Second Class Attribute Pool

* Can be obtained from specific element runes only.
* Can be equipped in the Rune Attribute (I) and (II) slots.
* Can be obtained at rune Level 1 and 5 only.

#### 4.2.1 Permanent ATK Boost (Wind Element Runes Only)

* Gain a permanent stack of **+50 to +120 ATK** after the first skill activation.
* Can stack with identical attributes. No known cap.
* Additional skill activations do not add more stacks.

#### 4.2.2 Skill Crit DMG (Lightning Element Runes Only)

* Gain **+14% to +30%** Crit DMG for the arcana skill.
* Can stack with identical attributes. No known cap.

#### 4.2.3 Skill DMG Increase (Water Element Runes Only)

* Gain **+10% to +15%** DMG for the arcana skill.
* Can stack with identical attributes. No known cap.

#### 4.2.4 Skill Crit Rate (Fire Element Runes Only)

* Gain **+5% to +25%** Crit Rate for the arcana skill.
* Can stack with identical attributes. No known cap.

### 4.3 Third Class Attribute Pool

* Can be obtained from all element runes.
* Can be equipped in the Rune Attribute (I) and (II) slots.
* Can be obtained at rune Level 1 and 5 only.

#### 4.3.1 Temporary ATK Boost (for 10 seconds)

* **Beast Bull, Blowfish, Divine Blessing:** Gain **+50 to +120 ATK** on skill activation.
* **Candle Dragon:** Gain **+6 to +20 ATK** per Fireball hit.
* **Kamaitachi:** Gain **+2 to +8 ATK** per Wind Blade hit.
* Can stack with identical attributes.
* Capped at **400 ATK** max for 10 seconds.

#### 4.3.2 HP Recovery / Instant Heal

* Recover **+50 to +120 HP** immediately upon activation.
* Can stack with identical attributes.
* Capped at **500 HP** per activation.

#### 4.3.3 HP Recovery Speed / Regeneration (for 10 seconds)

* Recover **+5 to +10 HP/s** continuously for 10 seconds.
* Can stack with identical attributes. No known cap.

#### 4.3.4 Effect Resistance

* Gain **+5% to +8%** debuff resistance.
* Can stack with identical attributes. No known cap.

## 5. Upgrading and Dismantling

### 5.1 Upgrade Costs

* Certain materials are used to level up runes.
* The material cost to level up a rune **increases with each level**.

### 5.2 Dismantle System and Refund Rate

* Unwanted or poorly rolled runes can be **dismantled** to obtain upgrade materials.
* Dismantling a **Lv. 1 rune** yields a **fixed base amount** of materials.
* Leveled-up runes return the base amount **plus 80% of the upgrade materials** spent on them.

## 6. FAQ

### 6.1 Activations and Countdowns

**Q: The skill activates "once per battle," but I've seen it activate more than once. Why?**
Once per battle is the default. Rune attributes can add bonus activations on top of it: the additional skill activation attribute and the skill activation on relay attribute.

**Q: What does a relay do to my skill?**
Your **1st relay** always restarts the countdown that is currently running, even without the skill activation on relay attribute. That attribute adds an instant activation on top of that. Your **2nd relay** does nothing.

**Q: What if I relay before the skill has activated for the first time?**
The first activation's countdown restarts. If you have the additional skill activation attribute, its countdown only starts after the first activation, so it isn't affected yet.

**Q: What if I relay after the first activation but before the additional activation?**
The relay restarts the additional activation's countdown.

**Q: What if I relay after the skill has used all its activations?**
Nothing to reset. A relay only triggers the skill if you have the skill activation on relay attribute.

**Q: Does the additional skill activation attribute have its own countdown?**
Yes. It starts only after the first activation, and it's a separate wait from the first one.

**Q: Do I need to meet the activation condition and wait for the countdown?**
Both are required. You can meet the condition before the countdown ends, but the skill only triggers when the countdown ends. If you can't meet the condition before the countdown ends, the skill activates as soon as you meet it.

**Q: Do I have to meet the condition again for the additional activation?**
Yes. The condition resets along with the countdown, so you must meet it again. The additional activation's countdown doesn't start until the skill has activated once. The skill activation on relay attribute ignores the condition.

**Q: When does the countdown start?**
The countdown starts when the Boss HP bar appears, as long as your Ninja is the correct element for the skill. Kamaitachi's weapon uses and Blowfish's damage taken both accumulate during the countdown. For Candle Dragon, taking damage pauses the countdown for 5s instead of resetting it.

**Q: Which skills can any Ninja use?**
Low-tier and mid-tier skills have no Ninja restriction, and neither does high-tier Divine Blessing. The other high-tier skills need a Ninja of the matching element.

**Q: What happens if my Ninja's element doesn't match the skill?**
The skill's countdown won't start without the correct element Ninja. The skill activation on relay attribute doesn't depend on a countdown, so it always triggers on your 1st relay, whatever the element of the starting Ninja or the relaying Ninja.

### 6.2 Runes and Slots

**Q: Do runes still help if I don't have a complete set?**
Only partly. Rune Stat (I) and (II) always work. Rune Attributes (I) and (II) stay off unless the arcana skill activates, so an incomplete or incorrect set gives you stats but no attributes.

**Q: Do Rune Stats only work for a Ninja of the matching element?**
No. Rune Stats work for any Ninja who is deployed at the time, whatever its element.

**Q: Can my Level 5 rune give a First Class attribute?**
No. Rune Attribute (II) only draws from Second and Third Class.

**Q: Can my Level 5 attribute be the same as my Level 1 attribute?**
Yes. Rune Attribute (II) can roll the same attribute as Rune Attribute (I), but only Second and Third Class attributes can be duplicated this way.

**Q: Does Rune Stat (II) get stronger when I level the rune past 8?**
No. It stays at what it rolled and does not increase with level.

### 6.3 Stacking and Caps

**Q: Can I stack identical attributes?**
Yes, for the Second and Third Class attributes listed with that rule. No cap is known for most of them. The exceptions are Temporary ATK Boost (capped at 400 ATK max for 10 seconds) and HP Recovery / Instant Heal (capped at 500 HP per activation).

**Q: Do bonus activations add more stacks of Permanent ATK Boost?**
No. Permanent ATK Boost only stacks once from the first skill activation.

**Q: Do Second Class bonuses boost my weapon or ninjutsu damage?**
No. Skill Crit DMG, Skill DMG Increase, and Skill Crit Rate apply to the arcana skill only.

### 6.4 Dismantling

**Q: How much do I get back from dismantling a rune?**
A Lv. 1 rune gives a fixed base amount of materials. A leveled-up rune gives that base amount plus 80% of the upgrade materials spent on it.

### 6.5 Data Notes

**Q: Are the ranges in this guide exact?**
The ranges are based on observation of thousands of runes. If you get values outside them, you are either very lucky or very unlucky.
